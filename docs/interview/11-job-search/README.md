# Job-Search Pipeline

Preparation and applications should overlap. The goal is a healthy pipeline by January, not a study plan that ends in January.

## Weekly pipeline

- Add suitable roles to a prioritized target list.
- Ask for focused referrals with a role link and concise fit summary.
- Submit tailored applications.
- Follow up on recruiter conversations.
- Record each stage and prepare specifically for the next one.
- Keep resume variants and recruiter answers consistent.

## Practical preparation

- Resume and LinkedIn consistency
- 30-second recruiter introduction
- Location, work authorization, and start-date answers stated accurately and concisely
- Compensation approach based on the role and market
- References and contact details ready when requested
- A document checklist for time-sensitive hiring steps

For immigration or employment-law decisions, use qualified professional advice rather than interview-preparation notes.
